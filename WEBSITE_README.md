# Ayurveda Wellness Clinic Website

A modern, professional, and mobile-friendly website for an Ayurveda clinic with bilingual support (English/Hindi), admin portal, and comprehensive features.

## 🌿 Features

### Core Pages
- **Home**: Hero section with clinic image, services preview, testimonials, FAQ, and CTA
- **About Us**: Clinic history, doctor profile, mission & vision, why choose us
- **Services**: Detailed treatment cards with benefits and booking options
  - Joint Pain
  - Sciatica
  - Cervical Pain
  - Back Pain
  - Skin Diseases
  - Psoriasis
  - Hair Fall
  - Weight Management
  - Diabetes Support
  - Panchakarma Therapy
- **Gallery**: Professional clinic photos with hover effects
- **Blog**: Health tips and Ayurveda articles
- **Appointment Booking**: Simple form with date selection
- **Contact**: Address, phone, email, WhatsApp, working hours, embedded Google Maps
- **Admin Portal**: Manage all clinic content

### Special Features
✅ **Bilingual Support**: English & Hindi with language switcher  
✅ **WhatsApp Integration**: Floating WhatsApp button for quick contact  
✅ **Google Maps**: Embedded location map on contact page  
✅ **FAQ Section**: Expandable FAQ component  
✅ **Admin Portal**: Edit clinic info, services, blog, gallery, testimonials, FAQs  
✅ **SEO Optimized**: Meta tags, sitemap, robots.txt, structured data  
✅ **Mobile Responsive**: Perfect on all devices  
✅ **Fast Loading**: Optimized images and lazy loading  
✅ **Smooth Animations**: Subtle transitions and hover effects  
✅ **Sticky Navigation**: Always accessible navigation bar  
✅ **Social Media Links**: Footer with social media integration  

## 🎨 Design

## 📱 Pages & Routes

| Page | Route | Features |
|------|-------|----------|
| Home | `/` | Hero, services, testimonials, FAQ |
| About | `/about` | Clinic history, doctor profile, mission/vision |
| Services | `/services` | All treatments with benefits |
| Gallery | `/gallery` | Clinic photos with hover effects |
| Blog | `/blog` | Health articles and tips |
| Appointment | `/appointment` | Booking form with date picker |
| Contact | `/contact` | Contact info, Google Maps, CTA |
| Admin | `/admin` | Content management portal |

## 🛠️ Admin Portal

Access at `/admin` to manage:
- **Clinic Information**: Name, address, phone, email, working hours, mission, vision
- **Doctor Profile**: Name, qualifications, experience, specialization
- **Services**: Add/edit treatment descriptions and benefits
- **Blog Posts**: Create and manage health articles
- **Testimonials**: Add patient reviews and ratings
- **FAQs**: Manage frequently asked questions
- **Gallery**: Upload and manage clinic photos

All changes are saved to browser's localStorage for persistence.

## 🌐 Bilingual Support

The website automatically detects and supports:
- **English (EN)**: Default language
- **हिंदी (HI)**: Hindi translations

Switch languages using the globe icon in the navigation bar.

## 📧 Contact Information

Update clinic details in the Admin Portal:
- Phone number (for "Call Now" button)
- WhatsApp number (for WhatsApp integration)
- Email address
- Physical address
- Working hours
